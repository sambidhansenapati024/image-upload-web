Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common CloudVault questions.

What file types can I upload?

CloudVault supports images, videos, PDFs, documents, archives and many other common file formats.

What is the maximum upload size?

The maximum upload size depends on your storage plan. This value will be displayed in your account settings.

How do I change my password?

Navigate to Security from your profile menu and select "Change Password".

Is my data secure?

Yes. Your files are securely stored and access is restricted to your account.
